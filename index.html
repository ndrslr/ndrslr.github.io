Research interests

Broadly, I'm interested in the non-perturbative structure of string theory and quantum gravity, and in the discrete data — symmetries, topological charges, defect groups — that low-energy effective field theory alone doesn't see. I'm currently working on two threads in Prof. Heckman's group.

Bordisms, TQFTs & the Swampland

I study how bordism groups constrain the non-perturbative structure of string/M-theory, in the spirit of the Swampland Cobordism Conjecture: global symmetries associated with duality bundles should be broken by new, singular objects, and bordism groups predict what those objects must be. [Add a sentence on your specific angle — e.g. which duality group, which dimension, which branes you're working out.]

Generalized symmetries in SCFTs

I'm also working on generalized (higher-form and non-invertible) symmetries of superconformal field theories engineered via string/M-theory — using branes wrapped on cycles of the internal geometry, and Symmetry TFTs, to extract the defect group and global structure of a given SCFT.
